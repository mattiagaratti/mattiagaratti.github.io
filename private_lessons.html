Private lessons

My goal is to make learning an effective and enjoyable experience!

Private Lessons for University and Secondary School Students

I offer private lessons tailored to students' individual needs, covering different ambits and topics.

  • University exam preparation: personalized support in Mathematics to help students be effectively prepared for exams, clarifing complex topics, and strengthening problem-solving skills.
  • Secondary school tutoring: focused lessons for the upper secondary school students, aimed at building a solid foundation in Mathematics and Physics through clear explanations and practical exercises.
  • Remedial exam assistance: specialized help for upper secondary school students with suspended judgment in Mathematics and Physics, offering targeted reviews and practice tests to ensure success.
  • Introduction to LaTeX: a beginner-friendly introduction to the LaTeX typesetting language, ideal for students who wish to produce professional-quality academic documents or theses.

Lessons are flexible and adapted to each student's learning style. For more information or to schedule a session, feel free to contact me!
